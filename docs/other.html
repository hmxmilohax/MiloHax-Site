Other Projects

Here are some other, smaller works of ours. They are not as feature-complete or "impressive" as some of our other stuff, but still worth checking out.

Guitar Hero 2 GYM & Guitar Hero 80s GYM

This is a love letter to Guitar Hero 2 and aims to be your new favorite way to experience vanilla Guitar Hero II and Guitar Hero Rocks the 80s in the modern era. Enjoy separate video and audio calibration, fixed HOPOs from GH80s along with built-in permadeath and randomizer options. Available for PS2 and Xbox 360 platforms, supports Guitar Hero 2, Guitar Hero 80’s, and even Guitar Hero 80s for 360 (see below).

Guitar Hero 80s - Xbox 360 Port

Created by jnack, this is a complete port of all of the songs, characters, venues, and menus from Guitar Hero Encore: Rocks the 80s, which only ever released on PS2.

Source Code (GitHub)

GH1 "Sekrit Edition"

Created by lunalawl, this is a small patch for Guitar Hero 1 that re-adds the two secret songs to the bonus setlist; "Trippolette" by Andraleia Buch and "Graveyard Shift" by Gurney.

📥 Download
Source Code (GitHub)

Amplitude "Space Edition"

Created by Carter (Squididly), this is a small patch for Amplitude (2003) that adds the demo-exclusive song, "Space" by P.O.D. This is not recommended for normal play as it extends loading times.

Associated Communities

Check out these communities that collaborate with us.

NeverHax

This is our sister community focused on modding the later Guitar Hero titles, which were mostly made by Neversoft. As they use a different engine and language, modding these games requires a different set of knowledge.

🔗 Link

Encore

Encore is a rhythm game that aims to include dedicated gamepad gameplay on top of full band implementation. It's built on top of a homegrown engine and is also available on Steam!

🎮 Steam

Source Code (GitHub)

Ultimate Mods

Home of Lego Rock Band Ultimate, the Ultimate mods community supports and modifies games that the rest of the Deluxe team did not want to. They have since gone above and beyond what was thought possible with these games.
